# Consumer-Behaviour-Analysis-During-Diwali-Festival-Using-Python

The analysis reveals that
•	Female customers dominate both purchase frequency and spending. 
•	Customers aged 26–35 years are the largest purchasing group. 
•	Married customers contribute significantly to sales. 
•	IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals are major buyers. 
•	Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi generate the highest revenue. 
•	Food, Clothing, and Electronics are the most popular product categories. 
This project demonstrates how Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) can transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The findings indicate that female customers aged 26–35 years, particularly married working professionals are the most valuable customer segment during the Diwali season. Additionally, states such as Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Delhi contribute the highest revenue, while Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics emerge as the most profitable product categories.
